# Release Gates

## Draft

- Pure deterministic engine and named fixtures exist.
- Tactical and Live clock policies use the same engine actions.
- Three active and three bench players are playable with rectangles.
- Puck state, Stamina, Pressure, Momentum, SHOOT, and turnover are legible.

## `v0.1.0-prototype`

Run 8–12 external playtests across both lanes.

- By the third shift, most testers can substitute and shoot without instruction.
- Testers can explain their turnover.
- Most intentionally pass at least one safe shooting opportunity.
- Fewer than 10% of failures are primarily blamed on controls or readability.
- Different rosters produce different rhythms.
- No repetitive swap pattern dominates every roster.
- Testers remember a clutch substitution or near-miss.

If players mostly replace the lowest Stamina bar without reasoning about the
puck or play state, the Prototype gate fails.

## `v0.5.0-alpha`

- A short run, opponents, construction, upgrades, saves, tutorial, representative
  UI, telemetry, and limited approved art exist.
- A player can begin and complete the run without developer guidance.

## `v0.8.0-beta`

- Launch features and content are complete.
- Both lanes are balanced separately.
- Accessibility, performance, device coverage, save migration, audio, and
  animation have completed dedicated passes.
- Work is limited to defects, clarity, balance, performance, and content finish.

## `v1.0.0`

- Stable release build, final onboarding, crash reporting, privacy/legal review,
  asset provenance, analytics, rollback, and versioned save compatibility pass.

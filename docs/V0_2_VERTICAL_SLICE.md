# SINBIN V0.2 Vertical Slice

## Purpose

Prove that a PC-first Live Shift can create understandable hockey chances and
that the same engine has a viable path to a genuine Coach Mode.

## In this slice

- 16:9 desktop-first prototype layout, while retaining the existing stage gate
  as a compatibility view.
- Visible Entry, Exit, and Stick information for every available player.
- Momentum as temporary offensive force, not an end-of-period target.
- Transparent, seeded goal chances and a visible scoreline.
- Pressure collapse as a goal against rather than an abstract score loss.
- Deterministic scenarios for a goal, a save, and a pressure collapse.

## Deliberately deferred

- Full Coach Mode action economy and opponent response content.
- Stick drafting, upgrading, and multiple equipment slots.
- Discipline accumulation, player removal, and short-handed formations.
- Named opponent archetypes, season progression, saves, character art, and
  commercial presentation.

## Acceptance questions

1. Can a new player identify why their chance is high or low before SHOOT?
2. Can they explain a save or goal against without developer help?
3. Does waiting after Shot Ready feel like a meaningful gamble rather than an
   automatic timer optimization?
4. Do displayed Entry, Exit, and Stick effects make a substitution plan easier
   to form?
